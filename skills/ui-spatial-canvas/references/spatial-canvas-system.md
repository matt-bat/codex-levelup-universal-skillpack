# Spatial Canvas System

Use this reference only after the Spatial Canvas activation condition in `SKILL.md` is satisfied.

## Interaction Modes

### Explore
1. Use framed transitions for browsing where they preserve content access.
2. Keep detail actions contextual and reversible.
3. Do not make endless scrolling the primary navigation model.

### Workstream
1. Keep high-frequency progress domains at the root.
2. Support fast triage with visible fallback controls.
3. Keep edit, assign, share, and approval actions near their active object.

### Operations
1. Keep high-frequency administrative domains flat and reachable.
2. Support dense management without hiding state or destructive consequences.
3. Offer expert shortcuts only alongside discoverable controls.

## Commerce

1. Avoid unnecessary storefront detours when an in-context transaction is safe and understandable.
2. Never reduce confirmation, pricing clarity, authentication, or recovery to save clicks.
3. Preserve platform and legal requirements over Spatial Canvas conventions.

## Visual Language

1. Use typography and spacing to establish hierarchy.
2. Use adaptive backgrounds only when legibility remains stable.
3. Use translucency, grain, depth, and motion only when they communicate focus or causality.
4. Respect reduced-motion and contrast preferences.

## Detailed Validation

1. Root domains and the home anchor remain immediately reachable.
2. Core flows do not gain unnecessary interaction steps.
3. Contextual actions have keyboard and non-gesture equivalents.
4. Loading, empty, error, success, and destructive states remain clear.
5. Content remains reachable on supported mobile and desktop viewports.
6. Focus order and focus restoration work through overlays or modalities.
7. Existing design tokens and component contracts remain intact.
8. Any exception is scoped to the affected surface and justified by a real constraint.
